# The Wall — public pages redesign (2026-10-20)

Third pass at the public pages. The second pass ("creator-platform structure, poster energy")
fell into the generic defaults the frontend-design skill lists: a near-black page with one
vermilion accent, zero-radius hairline layout, ALL-CAPS mono eyebrows on every section,
middle-dot meta strings, `→` on every link, 01/02/03 numbering on things that are not
sequences, and effects scattered across every section (WebGL threads, scramble text, click
sparks, spotlights, tilts, magnets, tickers). The owner's note: make it feel like a creators'
site with Kerala's vibe.

## Subject

Kerala's content creators and video editors finding each other. Audience: 18–30, on
Instagram, opening the link from a bio on a phone. The page's job: get them into the right
WhatsApp room, and let brands reach them.

## The idea: a Kerala wall

Every Kerala street has the same wall: lime-washed (with a spoon of *neelam*, the indigo
that makes it bluish rather than cream), a party's name hand-painted across it in letters a
metre tall, and film posters pasted over each other on top. It is where Kerala advertises
itself to itself. That is what a creators' room is, so the hero *is* that wall:

- **കൂട്ടം**, hand-painted across the wall in red at billboard size. This is the one
  memorable thing. It brushes in once, on load.
- The three rooms pasted onto it as **film posters** (creators on red paper, editors on lime,
  everyone else on mint), slightly crooked, overlapping, slapped on just after the paint.
- The headline is his line, in ink, at street scale.

Below the hero everything goes quiet: big ink type on the wall, generous space, no cards
for the sake of cards. Joining (`/join`, `/me`) stays the dark editing bay: you walk off the
street into the edit suite.

## Tokens

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Neelam wall | `#EEF2F8` | Page base: lime-wash with indigo, cool, never cream |
| Wall shade | `#E1E7F1` | Raised bands, inputs on the wall |
| Print ink | `#101828` | Text, the night bands (brands, footer) |
| Wall red | `#E4261C` | The wall-writing, primary buttons (existing rosso) |
| Poster yellow | `#F2B705` | Stars, the brief board (existing mustard) |
| Poster papers | lime `#C6F24E`, mint `#7FD4B8` | Lane posters only, always with ink text |

On wall pages the old token names are remapped (`[data-surface="wall"]`): `chalk` is the
foreground, `carbon` a raised panel, `graphite` a field, so shared components (directory,
form fields, FAQ) restyle themselves. Lime and mint are never text on the wall: only paper.

## Type

Two families, clearly distinct: **Anton** for everything that is painted or printed big
(headlines, poster titles, buttons), **Geist** for everything you read. No monospace and no
tracked-out caps labels on the wall pages; labels are sentence case. The Malayalam is a
Chromium-shaped image of Anek Malayalam (never a font download), roughened with an SVG
displacement filter so its edges look brushed.

Left aligned throughout, ragged right. Sections are separated by space, not rules. Numbers
appear only where the content is a sequence (the five joining steps, the Code's articles).

## Motion

One orchestrated moment: on load the wall-writing brushes in left to right, then the three
posters slap on (scale and rotate past, settle). Everything else moves only when touched:
posters lift on hover, FAQ answers open, the phone menu wipes down. No smooth-scroll
library, no WebGL, no scroll-scrubbed reveals. Reduced motion: the wall is simply painted.

## Review against the defaults

- Light page with one red accent would be the obvious "flip it light" move. What makes this
  specific: the indigo-white lime-wash (not paper white, not cream), the wall-writing at
  billboard scale in Malayalam, and pasted film posters. Kept.
- "Big number + small label" stats band: cut. The member count lives in one sentence under
  the buttons.
- Eyebrow chips above every heading: cut. Headings stand alone.
- Hub/beam diagram, holo card, pinned horizontal timeline, tickers: cut. Each was decoration
  standing in for content.
- `→` on links: cut, except where it means "go somewhere else" in running text.

## Outcome (built the same day)

- Built as planned. Two rounds of screenshot critique changed: the ൂ descender overlapped
  the headline (more space under the wall-writing); the first brush filter read as sandpaper
  (now noise stretched sideways, `0.01 0.4`, so the gaps look like a dragged brush); the
  lime-wash stripes repeated like pinstripe (two passes at unrelated widths); section padding
  was too generous; on phones the wall-writing was too small and the dock truncated.
- axe caught the red poster's small print at 90% opacity (white on red needs full opacity to
  pass 4.5:1).
- Removed: WebGL (Threads), GSAP, Lenis, every React Bits port, the scroll engine. Mobile LCP
  went from ~1.02s to ~0.89s.
- Not done, worth doing next: real photography from members' work in the posters (with
  permission), and the join flow (`/join`) still uses the older Editing Bay styling as
  intended, but its header could link back to the wall.
